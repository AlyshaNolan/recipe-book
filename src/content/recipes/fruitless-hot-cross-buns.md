---
title: Fruitless Hot Cross Buns
description: Spiced, fruitless, hot cross buns
ingredients:
    - 550g White Flour
    - 55g Caster Sugar
    - 1 Sachet Instant Yeast
    - 300ml Milk
    - 60g Melted Butter
    - 1 Egg
    - 1 Teaspoon Salt
    - 10g Mixed Spice
    - 65g White Flour
    - 80ml Water
    - 5g Caster Sugar

1. Melt butter, add milk and warm.

2. Add yeast, caster sugar, egg and mix. 

3. Add flour, mixed spice and salt and mix.

4. Knead on a lightly floured surface for 10-15 minutes untill smooth and elastic.

5. Rise for 1hr / until doubled in size.

6. Preheat oven to 180 fan bake.

7. Knead the dough for 1-2 minutes, shape buns and place in a lined tray.

8. Cover with a damp tea towel and leave to rise for 30mins.

9. Add flour, water and caster sugar together for the cross piping. Pipe crosses on buns.

10. Bake for 20mins or until golden brown. Once cooked, transfer to a wire rack.
